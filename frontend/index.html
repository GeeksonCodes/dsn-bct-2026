How It Works

1. Preference Profiling

Answer 3 simple questions about your product category, priorities (e.g. natural ingredients, budget), and ingredients to avoid.

2. RAG & Blended Matching

Our engine queries a 112k-item database utilizing a 50/50 semantic and popularity blend scoring configuration to fetch matches.

3. Multilingual Reranking

Gemini and ultra-fast fallbacks re-score the items, outputting friendly Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, or Igbo reasonings for value consciousness.
